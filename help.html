Help

How FletchShot works

FletchShot lives in your menu bar. The flow is three steps — shoot, mark up, hand off. Press ⌥⌘4 (changeable in Settings), drag over what you want, draw on it, then grab the tab at the bottom edge of the window and drop it anywhere.

First run

Permissions

Screen Recording permission

On first launch FletchShot shows a short guide. Click Open System Settings, allow FletchShot under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen Recording, then relaunch the app as the guide suggests.

Even after you allow it, macOS occasionally shows a dialog asking whether FletchShot may keep accessing your screen and audio. This is macOS behavior that applies to all screen-recording apps, not a FletchShot issue — choose to allow and carry on.

Microphone permission — only if you narrate recordings

The first time you turn the mic on for a screen recording, macOS asks for permission. To change it later, allow FletchShot under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

Captures fail or come out black?

That is almost always the Screen Recording permission. Check that it is granted, and relaunch the app after changing it.

Shoot

Capturing

Everything lives in the menu bar icon:

  • Select an area — or just press ⌥⌘4, then drag.
  • Repeat last area — re-shoot exactly the same spot. Great for before/after.
  • Full screen / Window — one click each.
  • Self-timer — capture after a short delay, for menus and hover states.
  • Open image… — annotate an existing image file.

Mark up

Annotating

Every capture opens in the annotation window. Eight tools, no sub-menus to study:

  • Arrow — the signature tapered arrow.
  • Text — click and type.
  • Shapes — rectangle, ellipse, straight line.
  • Draw — pen and highlighter.
  • Stamps — ✓ ✕ ? ! ♥.
  • Numbers — a counter stamp that increments on every click.
  • Mosaic — blur out what shouldn't be seen.
  • Crop — trim the capture.

Color and size are remembered per tool — set them once and they stay.

Hand off

Getting it where it needs to go

  • Drag tab — grab the tab at the bottom edge and drop straight into Slack, mail, a browser or a Finder folder.
  • Copy — ⌘C. Or ⇧⌘C to copy and close in one stroke.
  • Save — ⌘S. In Settings you choose: ask every time, or save silently into a fixed folder.
  • Send to Shortcuts — hand the result to a macOS Shortcut for your own automation.

Record

Screen recording and GIF

Choose Record screen from the menu bar. A control bar appears: drag to pick an area and press ●, or start immediately. Stop with ⌥⌘4 — the same key you shoot with.

After recording, switch between MP4 and GIF in the footer of the window. The mic is a single 🎤 toggle on the control bar; quality and mic volume live in Settings.

And three more

Pin, OCR, history

  • Pin (⌘P) — float the capture above every window while you work.
  • Recognize text — in the Edit menu. Copies the text inside the image to your clipboard.
  • Capture history — recent originals, reachable from the menu bar. Stored only on this Mac, never uploaded. Can be turned off in Settings.

Recipes

  • Re-shoot the same spot: Repeat last area → one arrow → drag tab into Slack. The second shot is where the speed shows.
  • Explain motion with a GIF: Record screen → ⌥⌘4 to stop → switch to GIF in the footer → drag tab into the chat.
  • Text from a screenshot: Capture a window → Recognize text → paste.

FAQ

Troubles, answered.

Captures fail or come out black.

Almost always the Screen Recording permission. See Permissions — grant it, then relaunch the app.

My voice isn't in the recording.

Two switches: the 🎤 toggle on the recording bar must be on, and FletchShot must be allowed under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

The GIF file is too big.

Lower the recording quality in Settings — the smallest setting trades detail for size. Shorter clips also help a lot.

Is anything uploaded anywhere?

No. Captures, recordings and history stay on this Mac. There is no account, no cloud, and the app makes no network calls with your content.

I found a bug / I have a question.

Write to support@fletchshot.app. A human reads it.
